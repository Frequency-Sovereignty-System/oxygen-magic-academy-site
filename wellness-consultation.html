Oxygen Magic Academy offers non-medical guided wellness consultation by appointment. These sessions are designed to support relaxation, personal wellness planning, lifestyle awareness, and recovery-oriented routines in a calm, human-centered environment.

Consultation Services

Service Approach

Each consultation is intended to help clients better understand their wellness needs, organize personal routines, and receive guided support for relaxation and recovery. Services are advisory and educational in nature and are not medical diagnosis, medical treatment, psychotherapy, or licensed healthcare service.

All services are provided as non-medical wellness guidance. Oxygen Magic Academy does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.
