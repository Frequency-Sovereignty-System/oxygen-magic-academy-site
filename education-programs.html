The Challenge Is No Longer Access to Answers

学校面对的问题，已经不只是学生能不能找到答案

Schools are being asked to respond to faster technology, changing student expectations, growing competition, and increasing pressure to demonstrate meaningful learning.

学校正在同时面对技术变化、学生期待变化、招生竞争，以及证明真实学习成果的压力。

01

Courses Look Increasingly Similar

课程越来越相似

Families often see the same promises across many schools. Institutions need a clearer reason to be chosen.

家长在不同学校看到的宣传越来越相似，学校需要更清楚地说明为什么值得被选择。

02

AI Makes Completion Easier

AI让完成任务更容易

A finished assignment no longer proves that a student understands, can explain, or can apply what was learned.

作业完成了，不再自然代表学生真正理解、能够表达或能够使用所学内容。

03

Teaching Value Is Hard to Show

教学价值难以被看见

Much of a teacher’s real work disappears after class, leaving schools with limited evidence of student growth.

教师大量真实工作在下课后消失，学校往往只剩下课表、出勤和分数。

A Partnership Built Around the School’s Real Need

从学校真正需要解决的问题开始合作

Oxygen Magic Academy does not ask a school to replace its existing curriculum, teachers, or identity. We begin with a defined educational need and explore a limited program that can be reviewed by the school before any broader commitment.

氧气魔法学院不会要求学校替换现有课程、教师或办学特色。合作从一个明确的教育问题开始，以有限范围开展，由学校根据真实反馈决定是否继续。

Start focused. Learn from real students. Expand only when it works.从小范围开始，用真实学生验证，有价值再继续。Program scope, responsibilities, materials, privacy, public communication, and any future licensing are confirmed separately in writing.项目范围、责任、材料、隐私、公开传播及未来可能的授权，均通过后续书面文件单独确认。

What a School Can Gain

学校可以获得什么

The goal is not to add technology for appearance. It is to help the school create clearer educational value for students, families, teachers, and future partners.

目标不是为了展示技术，而是帮助学校为学生、家长、教师和未来合作方形成更清楚的教育价值。

01

Stronger Enrollment Story

更清楚的招生理由

Present a more specific educational promise than class hours, textbooks, or general claims of quality.

不再只讲课时、教材和“优质教学”，而是让家长看到学生能够获得什么真实成长。

02

Visible Student Growth

看得见的学生成长

Explore ways for students to leave with meaningful work, clearer expression, and teacher-recognized progress.

让学生逐步形成有意义的作品、更清楚的表达，以及教师能够确认的成长记录。

03

Responsible AI Positioning

负责任的AI教育定位

Give teachers and families a clearer understanding of where AI may support learning and where human responsibility remains essential.

帮助教师和家长理解AI可以支持什么，以及哪些重要判断仍必须由人承担。

04

New Program Opportunities

新的课程机会

Explore short programs, curriculum enhancement, adult learning, career preparation, or other institution-specific formats.

可进一步讨论特色短期项目、现有课程升级、成人学习、职业准备或其他适合本校的形式。

05

Teacher Value That Remains Visible

让教师价值被保留下来

Support a learning environment in which teacher observation, feedback, and educational judgment remain important.

让教师的观察、反馈与教育判断继续成为课程中不可替代的重要部分。

06

A Path to Future Collaboration

为未来合作留下空间

Where a program proves valuable, the parties may separately discuss continued implementation, educator support, or institutional partnership.

当项目被真实验证有价值后，双方可再讨论持续实施、教师支持或更长期的机构合作。

A Clear Way to Begin

学校如何开始合作

The first conversation is simple and does not require a long-term commitment.

第一次沟通保持简单，不要求学校立即作出长期承诺。

Identify the Need

确认学校难题

学校根据实际反馈决定调整、继续、扩大或停止。
